  This session is the counter-example in the other direction: 17 new
  cases moved the exercised count by exactly 17, because each was built
  from a named unexercised rule rather than from a scenario.

  Stated as a fact about what the gate measures. NOT a proposal to
  change the gate. Current: 77 executed cases, 59 expected-fail, against
  minimums of 60 and 30.

6. THE UAE VALUES — CONFIRMED FROM PRIMARY SOURCE
--------------------------------------------------
  All three values this session was asked to confirm are CONFIRMED, each
  corroborated twice: once in the FTA/MoF prose and once in the @test
  bytes of the published PINT AE Schematron, which also fixes the UBL
  binding that the prose does not state.

  schemeID 0235 — CONFIRMED
    UAE-Electronic-Invoice-mandatory-fields V1.0 (23 Feb 2026), §4.1 and
    §4.2 row 12: "Seller electronic identifier — The identification of
    the Seller. For Businesses registered in the UAE, this will be a
    fixed value 0235."
    Binding, from published ibr-181-ae:
        not(cbc:EndpointID[@schemeID = "0235"]) or ...
    so it is cac:Party/cbc:EndpointID/@schemeID, IBT-034-1 on the seller
    and IBT-049-1 on the buyer. The endpoint VALUE is the TIN — same
    document, §4.1 row 11 and glossary row 11: "A unique 10-digit
    identifier and the first 10 digits of the 15-digit TRN".

  BTAE-15 = TL — CONFIRMED AS THE CORRECT VALUE FOR THESE FIXTURES,
                 WITH ONE CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF
    Binding, from published ibr-172-ae:
        cac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:CompanyID/@schemeAgencyID = "TL"
    The brief stated BTAE-15 "must be TL". The primary source does not
    say that. Mandatory-fields §4.1/§4.2 row 14 and published ibr-173-ae
    both give FOUR permitted values:
        TL  Commercial/Trade licence
        EID Emirates ID
        PAS Passport
        CD  Cabinet Decision
    TL is the correct choice for a trade-licensed business, which is what
    every UAE fixture models, and TL is what they now carry. It is not
    the only lawful value, and a rule asserting that it were would be
    wrong. Recorded because a validator that rejects a lawful EID- or
    PAS-identified seller is precisely the company-ending wrong answer of
    CLAUDE.md §4.1.
    Note also ibr-172-ae: when BTAE-15 is TL, the BTAE-12 authority name
    (@schemeAgencyName) MUST be present. The fixtures carry it.

  BTAE-02 = 8-character bitmask, 00000000 for standard — CONFIRMED
    Binding, from published ibr-154-ae:
        matches(cbc:ProfileExecutionID, "^[01]{8}$")
    Position order corroborated twice — the mandatory-fields §4.1 row 5
    list order, and the published transactiontype.gc code list, which
    names each position explicitly and agrees:
        1 Free trade zone       5 Continuous supply
        2 Deemed supply         6 Agent billing
        3 Margin scheme         7 Supply through e-commerce
        4 Summary invoice       8 Exports
    The corpus now exercises three of the eight positions with the
    dependent rules they switch on: 00000000 (standard, 24 fixtures),
    10000000 (free trade zone — pulls in BTAE-01 beneficiary ID via
    ibr-007-ae) and 00000001 (exports — pulls in the IBG-13 delivery
    address via ibr-152-ae).

  Additionally confirmed and applied, not asked for but load-bearing:
    ibr-132-ae   TRN is 15 digits, starts with 1, ends with 03
    ibr-128-ae   AE country subdivision is one of AUH DXB SHJ UAQ FUJ
                 AJM RAK
    aligned-ibrp-001-ae / -002-ae   the CustomizationID and ProfileID
                 values in section 2b

  buyer_identifier_missing_fail.xml omits the buyer VAT identifier

  published rules exist to require it; neither fires.

    ibr-135-ae  requires IBT-046 or IBT-048 only when the buyer endpoint

  an 8-character field) already recorded in RULES.md.

  🔒 diff. The correction is produced and held as
